Site24x7— strike it becomes OpManager Nexus cloud

The OpManager Nexus cloud mobile app help pages are a copy of the Site24x7 pages with the product name swapped. This report lists what changed, what still says Site24x7, and what the product team needs to confirm before the pages go live.

01
Summary

The verdict

✓ Done well

Page titles, meta descriptions, headings, role names and all 120 internal links now point to Nexus. 16 of 19 pages are word-for-word identical apart from the rename.

✕ Still Site24x7

4 text mentions, 9 links (App Store, Play Store, site24x7.com, support.site24x7.com) and 11 screenshots with Site24x7 branding.

? Needs a product call

14 claims copied from Site24x7, such as MSP and BU accounts, 130 locations, RUM and APM, and the Site24x7 app listing, may not be true for Nexus.

Fix these first

  1. 1
    High

    App Store and Play Store buttons open the Site24x7 app

    The listing is literally named "site24x7". Confirm this is the app Nexus users should install. If it is, say so on the page.

  2. 2
    High

    Device Key still says data goes "to site24x7.com"

    On both Admin pages. For a Nexus customer that reads as their data going to another product.

  3. 3
    High

    Nothing links to the iOS release notes

    The iOS overview's link was replaced by an inline iOS 26 list, so the release notes and the Latest iOS features pages can't be reached from the iOS help page.

  4. 4
    Medium

    11 screenshots show Site24x7 branding

    They include the welcome screens, the iOS 27 widgets, notification labels and the IT Automation prompt. Retake them if there's a Nexus-branded build.

  5. 5
    Medium

    No Site24x7 copy fixes were carried across

    All 23 known typos, including "global.template", came across unfixed. Fix them once in both sites.
